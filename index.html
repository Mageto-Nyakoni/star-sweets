Hi, I'm Stella.

I started Star Sweets out of my tiny apartment kitchen in 2025, armed with a hand mixer, a well-worn copy of Bouchon Bakery, and an embarrassing number of butter wrappers.

Every recipe here is tested until it's genuinely unforgettable — whether that's a Wednesday-night brownie or a weekend croissant project.

Pull up a chair. There's always something in the oven.
